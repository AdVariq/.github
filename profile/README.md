## 🎯 What is AdVariq?

[**AdVariq**](https://advariq.com) is an AI static ad generator and creative workflow platform designed specifically for performance marketing and e-commerce growth. 

Most e-commerce teams know what winning ads look like, but producing enough creative variations to fight **ad fatigue** is slow, expensive, and repetitive. AdVariq bridges the gap between raw AI image generation and real-world media buying—combining **battle-tested advertising frameworks** with automated layout, copy hierarchy, and product synthesis so marketers can launch high-ROAS campaigns faster.
